About the data

Met Éireann’s 1 km monthly rainfall grids for 2020–2025, averaged per calendar month, then averaged over the 1 km cells whose centres fall in each hexagon. Coastal hexagons are drawn whole; the few that are almost all sea (headland tips, islets) are left out. In the average, rain is spread evenly within each month, since those data are monthly totals. The 2025 view uses Met Éireann’s 1 km daily rainfall grid for 2025, averaged into the same hexagons, so it shows individual storms. Switching keeps the date, so you can pause on a day and compare.

Northern Ireland (grey) isn’t in Met Éireann’s grid.
